Overview

RetroGame is a head-to-head Tetris that runs in the browser. You play against an AI opponent, and instead of a keyboard you control your pieces with your hand in front of a webcam. A BBC micro:bit adds physical controls and sound: shake it to pause, and it beeps when you clear a row, win, lose or reach a new theme.

How it works

  • Hand tracking: MediaPipe Hands follows your hand through the camera. Rotating your fingers rotates the piece, moving your hand left or right shifts it, pushing down speeds it up, and a quick downward flick drops it. The keyboard works as a fallback.
  • AI opponent: it scores every possible placement by stack height, lines cleared, holes and bumpiness, then moves its piece step by step so you can watch it play. Beginner, Intermediate and Expert levels change how fast pieces fall.
  • micro:bit: a MicroPython program on the micro:bit talks to the game over USB using the browser's Web Serial API.
  • Rounds and music: each round switches between five visual themes (classic, neon, forest, sunset and mono), with music for every level and the main menu.

Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript; MediaPipe Hands; the Web Serial API; MicroPython on a micro:bit v2.
