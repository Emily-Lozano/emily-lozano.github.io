Overview

This is the draft KrayonCAD design I contributed to for my school's FRC robotics team in 2025. After the game for that year's competition, Reefscape, was released, my team held a two-day kickoff to discuss game strategy and robot designs. After a long discussion, we made this very preliminary first design in KrayonCAD on Onshape.

The design

The robot we hoped to build had four major components:

  • A three-stage cascade elevator.
  • A reorienting mechanism that turns the cylinder (coral) to the vertical axis.
  • An intake made specifically for the cylinder (coral).
  • An intake for the ball (algae), built around a roller.
